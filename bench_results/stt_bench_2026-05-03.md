# STT bench — 2026-05-03

**Device:** Pixel 6a (Tensor G1, 6 GB RAM), Android `CP1A.260405.005`
**Build:** Flutter 3.41.7 profile mode
**Fixture:** `test_data/record_out_16k.wav` — 59.94s of speech, 16 kHz mono PCM16
**Model:** NEMO Parakeet TDT 0.6B int8 (offline, what's bundled in `assets/models/parakeet/`)
**Method:** 1 untimed warmup + 5 timed iterations per config, p50/p95 over the 5

## Results

| Config | p50 | p95 | mean | min | max | cold_load | RTF (p50) | RSS Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CPU, 2 threads (production) | **9070** | 9160 | 8981 | 8746 | 9160 | 3769 ms | 0.151× (6.6× realtime) | 877 → 1235 MB |
| CPU, 4 threads | **6833** | 8921 | 7235 | 6646 | 8921 | 3549 ms | 0.114× (8.8× realtime) | 899 → 1271 MB |
| NNAPI, 4 threads | **7035** | 7260 | 7052 | 6840 | 7260 | 4080 ms | 0.117× (8.5× realtime) | 876 → 1216 MB |

All three configs produced **identical transcripts** — accuracy is preserved across providers/threads.
Sample transcript head: `"Okay, so today we are testing out this at late night. This is Sunday. Branding is hustling..."`

## Findings

1. **Offline batch transcription is fundamentally incompatible with the 500ms stop-to-home target.**
   Best p50 is 6.83s for a 60s clip — **13.6× over budget.** No CPU/EP knob closes that gap.

2. **NNAPI provides ~no benefit on NEMO Parakeet TDT.**
   1.29× over CPU/2 threads, essentially the same as bumping to 4 threads (1.33×). The NEMO transducer
   has ops that fall back to CPU under NNAPI, so the EP just adds compilation overhead (cold load
   3549 → 4080 ms) for marginal gain. **Skip NNAPI for this specific model.**

3. **CPU/4 threads is unstable under sustained load.**
   p95 (8921) is 30% above p50 (6833) — almost certainly thermal throttling on iteration 5. NNAPI
   was tighter (p95 only 3% above p50). Real-world recordings are spaced apart, so this matters less
   in production than in a back-to-back bench, but worth flagging.

4. **Cold load is 3.5–4 seconds.**
   Whatever recognizer we ship, this has to be paid in `addPostFrameCallback` at app boot — never
   on first recording. Otherwise first record session feels like a 4-second freeze.

5. **Memory delta is ~340–400 MB.**
   Acceptable on 6 GB. The 877 → 1235 MB jump is mostly Dart heap + sherpa-onnx tensors; the 622 MB
   encoder is mmap'd, not duplicated into anonymous RAM.

## Architecture implications

- **Streaming model is now data-validated**, not just expected. Whatever streaming variant we pick
  (zipformer, NEMO streaming, etc.), the win is *not* "make inference faster" — it's "do the work
  *during* the recording so the stop path has nothing left to do." Even if the streaming model is
  slower per-second of audio, the user-visible latency drops near zero.

- **Don't add NNAPI to the offline path.** Re-test it on the streaming model — Zipformer has a
  different op profile and may benefit more.

- **Bump production `numThreads` from 2 → 4.** Free 1.33× speedup, identical transcripts. The
  thermal variance is acceptable for spaced recordings.

- **Pre-load STT at app boot.** 3.5s cold-load belongs in app warmup, not user-perceived latency.

## Limitations / what we didn't measure

- Single device (Pixel 6a). Tensor G1 is mid-tier; flagship Snapdragon will be faster CPU,
  potentially much faster NNAPI. iPhone CoreML is unmeasured.
- One audio fixture. WER vs reference text not computed (no reference text was supplied with the
  fixture). Latency-only data.
- 5 iterations is enough for p50 but noisy for p95. Run with `iterations: 20` for stable tail
  numbers if shipping a regression suite.
- Streaming model not benched yet (no streaming model bundle on disk).
- Gemma + e5 not benched yet.
